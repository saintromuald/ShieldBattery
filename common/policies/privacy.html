HyperShield TEST SERVICE — Privacy Policy

This policy describes data handling in this HyperShield test deployment, not a reviewed public production privacy policy. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Information stored by the test service

  • Account details such as username, email address, password hash, and account settings.
  • Content and activity you submit, including chat messages, game results, ratings, replays, uploaded images, reports, and moderation records.
  • Technical and security records, which may include IP addresses, client version, session identifiers, error details, and timestamps.

The desktop client also keeps settings, caches, replay-library information, and logs on your device. Local material remains on your device unless you upload it, submit a bug report, or use a feature that synchronizes it. Clearing local client data or uninstalling the app does not delete information already stored by the server.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts and gameplay features, remember preferences, maintain security, diagnose problems, moderate conduct, and improve the test. Session cookies or similar local storage may keep you signed in. HyperShield does not sell account data or use it for third-party advertising profiles.

Storage and access

Account and uploaded data are stored in this deployment's test database and file storage, which may be local infrastructure rather than a durable production service. Access is limited to the administrator and people or service providers needed to operate, secure, or troubleshoot the deployment. Email or hosting providers may process data if the administrator enables them. Their own terms may also apply.

Test storage may be inspected, backed up, reset, moved, or deleted as part of development. No particular retention period, backup schedule, or production security assurance is promised. Avoid submitting sensitive, confidential, or irreplaceable information. Bug-report logs can contain paths, system details, and recent client activity; review what you submit.

Your choices

You may edit available account settings, avoid optional uploads, clear local client data, or stop using the service. You may ask the administrator for access, correction, export, or deletion of account data. Requests may be limited by technical constraints, security and moderation needs, applicable law, or a test-environment reset. Public content you posted may have been seen or copied by other users before removal.

The service is not intended for children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under 13.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the test evolves; the date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, contact the administrator of this HyperShield deployment through the support method or official channel they provide.

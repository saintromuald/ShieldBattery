HyperShield TEST SERVICE — Acceptable Use Policy

These rules apply to this HyperShield test deployment and are not a reviewed production legal policy. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Respect other users

Do not use HyperShield to:

  • Harass, threaten, dox, or target another person, including with hateful or sexual abuse.
  • Post unlawful, exploitative, or non-consensual content, or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Spam, advertise without permission, impersonate others, or misrepresent administrator status.
  • Cheat, grief, deliberately disrupt matches, manipulate rankings, or evade a restriction or ban.

Protect the service and its data

Do not:

  • Access another account, private area, or non-public data without authorization.
  • Bypass security, rate limits, permissions, client checks, or other access controls.
  • Distribute malware, interfere with infrastructure, or attempt denial-of-service activity.
  • Collect user data at scale or scrape private information without permission.
  • Upload content that infringes another person's copyright, privacy, or other rights.

Security and compatibility testing must be authorized by the administrator and limited to the agreed test scope. Test clients and tools must not disrupt other users or expose credentials, private data, or unreleased content.

Reports and enforcement

The administrator may remove content, restrict features, or suspend access to investigate abuse, protect the deployment, or comply with law. Test-service enforcement may occur without prior notice. Relevant account, gameplay, report, and technical records may be reviewed as described in the Privacy Policy.

Report violations through the in-app reporting tools or contact the administrator of this HyperShield deployment through the support method or official channel they provide.
